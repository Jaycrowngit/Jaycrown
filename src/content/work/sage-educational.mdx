## The problem

Schools needed to run timed exams online, at scale, for students on patchy mobile connections, without answers leaking or sessions dropping mid-exam.

## My role

Full-stack engineer across the API, the Next.js admin portal and the Flutter student app.

## Architecture

- **Laravel API** for auth, scheduling and results; **Python** services for scoring and analytics.
- **MongoDB** for flexible question banks (multiple choice, theory, media).
- **Next.js admin portal** for setting exams and reviewing results.
- **Flutter student app** that caches the exam locally and syncs answers, so a dropped connection doesn't lose work.

## Key decisions

- **Offline-tolerant exam sessions** with server-side timing as the source of truth.
- **Split services by workload:** request/response in Laravel, heavier scoring in Python.
