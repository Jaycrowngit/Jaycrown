## The problem

Utility payments in Nigeria run on a chain of upstream providers (payment gateways, telcos and electricity distributors), each with its own latency, failure modes and settlement timing. Users expect airtime to land in seconds; the business needs every naira accounted for.

## My role

Technical Team Lead. I lead full-stack engineering, own the **Monnify** gateway integration and its webhook pipeline, and am accountable for platform reliability.

## Architecture

- **Funding:** dedicated virtual accounts per user via Monnify; inbound transfers confirmed by signed webhooks.
- **Fulfilment:** purchases move through an explicit state machine (`pending → processing → fulfilled | failed | reversed`), so no request is ever lost.
- **Reconciliation:** a scheduled job compares provider reports against the internal ledger and flags discrepancies before customers notice.

## Key decisions

- **Idempotency first.** Providers retry webhooks; every event is keyed and processed exactly once.
- **Ledger, not balances.** Wallet balances are derived from immutable ledger entries, which makes disputes auditable.
- **Fail loudly upstream, gracefully downstream.** Provider outages trigger automatic reversal and a clear message instead of a stuck spinner.
