## The problem

Converting crypto to Naira was slow and trust-heavy. Users needed transparent rates and fast, reliable bank payouts.

## My role

Full-stack engineer on the mobile app and the backend exchange and payout flows.

## Architecture

- Live **rate tracking** with a locked quote window per trade.
- **Wallet processing** that confirms deposits before releasing funds.
- **Automated payouts** to Nigerian bank accounts via payment gateways.

## Key decisions

- **Quote locking** so users never see a different rate at confirmation.
- **Manual-review lane** for unusual transactions instead of blocking all users.
